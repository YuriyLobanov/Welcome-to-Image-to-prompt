How to change the background of a photo, step by step

  1. Start with a sharp photo. The subject should be in focus and not cut off at the edges. Busy or blurry backgrounds are fine; the AI replaces them.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and attach the photo with the + button.
  3. Paste your prompt from the builder and send it.
  4. Check the edges. Look at hair, glasses, fingers and the outline of the product. If something looks cut out, reply “blend the edges of the hair more naturally”.
  5. Check the light. If the subject looks pasted in, reply “match the light on me to the new background”.

Why some AI background changes look fake

An AI background changer swaps the pixels behind you in seconds. What gives it away is everything around the edges. Ask for these four things and the result looks like one photo, not two:

  • Matching light. Sunset light is warm and low; office light is soft and cool. If your face is lit from the left, the new scene should be too.
  • A shadow on the ground. A person or product with no shadow looks like it is floating.
  • The right horizon. A photo taken from below needs a background seen from below. Mention “the horizon at the right height for my camera angle”.
  • Soft hair edges. Flyaway hair, glasses and fur are where cut-outs fail. Always ask the AI to keep “the edges of my hair”.

Change the background of a product photo

For online stores, choose “A product” and “Pure white” in the builder. Marketplaces such as Amazon ask for a pure white background on the main product image, so the prompt asks for #FFFFFF with no gradient.

  • Check the label and logo letter by letter. AI models sometimes redraw small text; if they do, reply “keep the label text exactly as in my photo”.
  • Keep reflections honest. Glass and metal reflect their surroundings; ask for “neutral studio reflections” after the change.
  • Use the same prompt for every product so your whole catalog has matching light and shadows.

Questions

Can ChatGPT change the background of a photo?
Yes. Upload the photo, paste a prompt that asks it to replace only the background and keep the subject unchanged, and send it. ChatGPT returns a new image with the new background.
Can Gemini change the background of a photo?
Yes. Gemini's image model (Nano Banana) edits uploaded photos. Start the prompt with “Edit this photo” so it changes your photo instead of creating a new one.
How do I make the background of a photo white?
Choose “Pure white” in the builder. The prompt asks for a #FFFFFF background with no gradient and a soft shadow under the subject, which is what most online stores expect.
Will the AI change my face?
It can, slightly. The prompts on this page ask the AI to keep your face, hair and clothing exactly as they are. If something changes, reply “keep my face identical to the original photo” in the same chat.
Can I just blur the background instead of replacing it?
Yes. Tick “Blur the new background”, or ask for “the same background, softly out of focus, like a portrait lens”.
