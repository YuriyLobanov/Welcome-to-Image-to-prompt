How to make an AI Christmas photo

  1. Choose one sharp photo. Faces toward the camera, even light, nobody cut off. For a group, use a single photo with everyone in it.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and attach the photo with the + button.
  3. Paste your prompt from the builder and send it.
  4. Fix details in the same chat. Try “warmer light”, “more snow” or “keep the faces identical to my photo”.
  5. Download the image and share it or print it as a card.

Turn it into an AI Christmas card

Tick “Make it a card” in the builder and type your greeting. The prompt asks for a printed card layout with your words in the lower part of the image.

  • Keep the greeting short. Under eight words prints cleanly; long sentences are where AI spelling mistakes appear.
  • Check every letter before you print. If a word is wrong, reply “fix the spelling of …” in the same chat.
  • For a 5×7 print, add “vertical 5:7 format” to the prompt.

Tips for a realistic result

  • Ask for real light, not “beautiful” light. Window light, string lights or overcast snow light look like a photo; vague praise words give a glossy, plastic look.
  • Keep “natural skin texture” in the prompt. It stops the airbrushed effect.
  • Don't change too much at once. Swap the setting first, then the outfits in a second message.
  • Mention the number of people in group photos so nobody is added or dropped.

Questions

Which AI Christmas photo generator should I use?
ChatGPT and Gemini both edit your own photos well. Upload your photo, paste a prompt from this page and send it. If one of them changes the faces, try the same prompt in the other.
Can I make a Christmas card with AI?
Yes. Tick “Make it a card” in the builder, type your greeting and the prompt asks for a printed card layout with your text. Check the spelling in the result, because AI models still misspell words now and then.
How do I keep everyone's faces the same?
Use one sharp photo where every face is visible and well lit, keep the sentence that asks the AI to preserve exact faces, and ask it not to add or remove people. If a face drifts, reply “keep the faces identical to my photo” in the same chat.
Can I put my dog or cat in a Christmas photo?
Yes. Choose the pet look, or pick “Me and my pet” and upload one photo where both of you are visible. For painted styles, see the AI pet portrait guide.
Is it safe to upload family photos?
Your photo goes to the AI service you choose, not to this site. Check that service's privacy settings, and ask the people in the photo before you upload it.
