How to make a pet portrait from a photo

  1. Pick your best photo. Eyes in focus, daylight or soft indoor light, the whole head in frame. Photos taken at your pet's eye level work best.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and attach the photo with the + button.
  3. Paste your prompt from the builder and send it.
  4. Compare with the original. If an ear, a spot or the eye color is off, reply with exactly what to fix, for example “the white patch is on the left side of the chest”.
  5. Download the result and keep the largest version for printing.

Keep it looking like your pet

AI tends to turn every dog into a generic version of its breed. These details keep the portrait recognisable:

  • Name the markings. “White blaze on the nose”, “one brown ear”, “black spot over the left eye”. The prompt asks the AI to keep them, but naming them helps more.
  • Mention eye color and ear shape if they are unusual for the breed.
  • Use one pet per photo for single portraits. For two pets, pick a photo where both faces are clear.
  • Change one thing at a time. Get the style right first, then ask for the costume or the frame.

Print it or give it as a gift

  • Square or 4:5 works for most frames. Add “square format” or “vertical 4:5 format” to the prompt.
  • Download the largest version the tool offers. For prints larger than 8×10 inches, run it through an image upscaler first.
  • Tick “Show it framed on a wall” in the builder to preview how it looks in a gold frame before you order a print.

Questions

Which AI pet portrait generator should I use?
ChatGPT and Gemini both edit your own pet photos well. Upload the photo, paste a prompt from this page and send it. If one of them changes your pet's face or markings, try the same prompt in the other.
Does it work for cats and other pets?
Yes. The default prompts are written for an AI dog portrait; choose “My cat” in the builder and the prompt keeps the cat's face, eye color and coat pattern. For rabbits, birds and other pets, use the dog prompt and replace the word “dog”.
Can I be in the portrait with my dog?
Yes. Choose “Me and my pet” and upload one photo where both of you are visible. The prompt asks the AI to keep both faces.
How do I make a Renaissance pet portrait?
Pick the Renaissance noble style, upload your pet's photo and send the prompt. It dresses your pet in a velvet cloak and lace collar and paints it in the style of a 17th-century oil portrait.
Can I print an AI pet portrait?
Yes, for small and medium prints. Download the largest version and upscale it for anything bigger than 8×10 inches. Check the eyes and whiskers at full size before you order.
